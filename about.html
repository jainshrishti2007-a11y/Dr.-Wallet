ABOUT DR. WALLET

Built by a student who kept losing track of ₹100 coffees.

Dr. Wallet started as a way to stop guessing where the month's allowance went — now it's a small, honest tool for anyone tired of budgeting apps built for salaries, not students.

THE STORY

Every app felt like it was built for someone else's money.

Most expense trackers assume a salary, an office commute and a fixed monthly rent. Student life doesn't look like that — it's an allowance that has to stretch, a mess bill that hits all at once, and the same ₹100 coffee that quietly adds up without ever feeling like "real spending."

Dr. Wallet was built to fit that reality instead of fighting it — fast enough to log an expense between classes, and smart enough to notice when a habit (hello, daily chai) deserves its own spotlight.

HOW IT WORKS

WHAT WE CARE ABOUT

The principles behind Dr. Wallet

Fast, Not Fussy

If logging an expense takes longer than buying it, the app has failed. Everything is built around speed.

Private by Design

Your data lives in your own browser. No servers, no accounts sold, nothing tracked behind your back.

Made for Student Life

Mess fees, hostel rent, fest budgets — the categories and defaults are built around how students actually spend.

Free, Always

No premium tier, no paywalled charts. A budgeting tool shouldn't cost the very money it's helping you save.
