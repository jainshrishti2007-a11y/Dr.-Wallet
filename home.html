ABOUT DR. WALLET

Made for students, not corporate expense reports.

Most expense apps are built for salaries, invoices and tax season. Dr. Wallet is built around allowance days, split hostel bills and the last week of the month when the wallet always runs thin.

Log a spend in seconds, set a monthly budget that resets automatically, and see exactly which habit is quietly draining your balance — all from one clean dashboard.

FEATURES

Everything a student budget actually needs

Log a Spend in Seconds

Add an expense with an amount, a category and a note — no forms, no friction, built for between-class moments.

Monthly Budget Goals

Set a limit per category — mess, transit, fun money — and watch the progress bar so you know before you overspend, not after.

Visual Spending Reports

A weekly breakdown that shows exactly where your allowance disappeared, without digging through a bank statement.

Overspend Alerts

Get nudged the moment a category creeps past 80% of budget, so the last week of the month stops being a surprise.

Logging Streaks

A small daily habit — logging streaks keep you honest about tracking, the same way they keep you honest about spending.

Savings Goals

Set aside money for a trip, a gadget or an emergency fund, and track it right alongside your everyday spending.

THE TRACKER

A ledger that actually reads like your life

Every entry lands in a clean, chronological ledger — so "where did my money go" is answered in one glance, not one spreadsheet.

  • Categorised automatically as food, transit, books, hostel & more
  • Color-coded so overspending is obvious at a glance
  • Synced to your monthly budget in real time
