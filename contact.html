FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Wallet free?

Yes — completely free, with no premium tier. Budgeting shouldn't cost the money it's helping you save.

Is my data secure?

Your budget and expenses are stored locally in your own browser (localStorage), tied to your account. Nothing is sent to a server, and no one else can see it.

Will my data sync across devices?

Not yet — since everything is stored in your browser rather than a server, your data currently stays on the device you added it from. Cross-device sync is on the roadmap.

Can I edit or delete my expenses later?

Yes. Every transaction in your dashboard ledger has a delete button, and you can update your budget anytime from the "Edit Budget" option.

How does the "Hot Topics" feature work?

If you log the same item three or more times — even typed slightly differently, like "coff" and "coffee" — Dr. Wallet groups them and surfaces it as a one-tap quick-add chip, so recurring buys take a single tap instead of a full form.

Can I use this on mobile?

Absolutely. Dr. Wallet is fully responsive and works across phones, tablets and desktops.

Do I need to make an account?

Yes — a free account lets Dr. Wallet keep your budget and expenses tied to you specifically, rather than mixed up with anyone else using the same browser.
